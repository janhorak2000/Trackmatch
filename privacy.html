PaceDuel Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PaceDuel ("PaceDuel", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the PaceDuel mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

PaceDuel is a running application that allows users to participate in running challenges and races against other users. To provide these features, PaceDuel may process account information, GPS/location data, running activity data, photographs/selfies, and information obtained through Facebook Login.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PaceDuel, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Facebook Login Information

If you choose to create or access your PaceDuel account using Facebook Login, we may receive information made available to us by Facebook through the permissions you authorize.

This may include:

  • Your Facebook user ID or account identifier
  • Your name
  • Your email address, where made available by Facebook and authorized by you
  • Your Facebook profile picture, where made available and authorized

We use this information to create and maintain your PaceDuel account, authenticate you, identify your account, and provide the Service.

We do not use Facebook Login information for purposes unrelated to providing the PaceDuel Service unless we obtain your permission or are otherwise legally permitted to do so.

2.2 GPS and Location Data

PaceDuel may request access to your device's location services while you participate in running activities.

GPS/location data may include information such as your geographic position, route, distance, speed, pace, elevation, timestamps, and other information calculated from your running activity.

We use GPS data to:

  • Track and record your running activity
  • Calculate distance, pace, speed, and other running statistics
  • Enable head-to-head running competitions
  • Display relevant running activity to you and, where applicable, other participants
  • Determine competition results and rankings
  • Improve and maintain the Service
  • Detect abuse, manipulation, cheating, or fraudulent activity

PaceDuel only requests location permissions that are reasonably necessary for the running functionality of the application.

2.3 Camera and Selfie Data

PaceDuel may request access to your device camera if you choose to take or upload a profile photograph, selfie, or other photograph within the Service.

We use photographs you provide to display your profile and to support features of the PaceDuel Service.

We do not use your photographs for facial recognition or to create a biometric identification profile unless we clearly inform you of such processing and obtain any consent or other legal authorization required by applicable law.

Camera access is not required merely to use PaceDuel's running functionality unless a particular feature specifically requires it.

2.4 Running and Activity Data

We may collect information generated through your use of PaceDuel, including:

  • Running distance
  • Running time
  • Pace and speed
  • Routes and GPS coordinates
  • Race and challenge results
  • Competition history
  • Achievements and rankings
  • Dates and times of running activities
  • Information you choose to add to your PaceDuel profile

2.5 Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information when you use PaceDuel, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • Unique device or installation identifiers
  • IP address
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Basic information about application performance

This information helps us operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve PaceDuel.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We may use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your PaceDuel account
  • Authenticating your identity and allowing you to log in
  • Tracking and recording running activities
  • Providing head-to-head running races and challenges
  • Calculating race results, rankings, pace, distance, and statistics
  • Displaying your profile and relevant activity to other users
  • Preventing cheating, abuse, fraud, and unauthorized use
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the Service
  • Providing customer support
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving the application
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations
  • Protecting our rights, users, and property

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") applies, we process your personal data on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: processing necessary to provide PaceDuel features that you request, including account creation, authentication, running tracking, and competitions.
  • Consent: where you voluntarily give permission for processing, such as certain device permissions or other processing where consent is legally required.
  • Legitimate interests: where necessary to operate, secure, improve, and protect PaceDuel, provided that our interests do not override your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws or lawful requests.

The specific legal basis may depend on the particular processing activity.

PaceDuel is designed around competition between runners. Certain information may therefore be visible to other users depending on the features you use and your account settings.

This may include:

  • Display name

  • Race results
  • Running statistics

8. GPS Data and Privacy

GPS information can reveal sensitive information about where you run and may potentially reveal places you frequently visit.

We therefore recommend that you consider the privacy implications before sharing running routes or other activity information with other users.

Where technically feasible, PaceDuel may apply measures designed to reduce unnecessary exposure of precise location information.
